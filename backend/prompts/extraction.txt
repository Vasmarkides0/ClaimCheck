You are a senior technical due diligence analyst at a top VC firm.
I'm going to give you text extracted from a startup's pitch deck.

YOUR JOB: Identify technically ambiguous claims that require expert verification — claims where the truth is NOT obvious and a technical analyst would need to fact-check carefully.

WHAT COUNTS AS A CLAIM WORTH EXTRACTING:
- Performance benchmarks against named competitors: "outperforms GPT-4 on MMLU", "2x better than OpenAI's Whisper"
- Accuracy claims on specific datasets or benchmarks: "95% accuracy on ImageNet", "state-of-the-art on SWE-bench"
- Speed comparisons to specific hardware or systems: "3x faster than H100", "runs inference in 10ms vs 40ms for competitor X"
- "First to market" or "only solution" assertions that could be technically disputed
- Cost or efficiency claims with specific numbers: "80% cheaper than GPT-4", "$0.001 per 1M tokens", "10x more energy efficient"

EXPLICITLY IGNORE — DO NOT EXTRACT THESE:
- Obvious facts that anyone can verify without technical knowledge
- Team size, headcount, or org structure claims
- Funding amounts, valuations, or investment history
- Timelines, roadmaps, or projected milestones
- Vague marketing language: "world-class", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary", "best-in-class"
- Opinions or future projections: "we believe the market will...", "we plan to..."
- Business model claims, revenue figures, or customer counts
- Any claim that does not require technical domain knowledge to evaluate

Return a MAXIMUM of 5 claims, prioritising the most technically ambiguous and verifiable ones. These should be claims a VC technical analyst would specifically want to fact-check.

For each claim, return:
1. "original_text" — the exact quote from the deck
2. "claim_type" — one of: performance_benchmark, speed_comparison, accuracy_claim, market_position, cost_claim, scale_claim
3. "key_entities" — the specific models, products, benchmarks, datasets, or numbers mentioned
4. "search_queries" — exactly 3 Google search queries that would help verify this claim. Make them specific and targeted.

IMPORTANT: Return ONLY valid JSON, no markdown, no explanation. Just the JSON:
